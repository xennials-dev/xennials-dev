Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Xennials automation architectures.

How quickly can Xennials deploy automated workflows for our business?

Most core automation pipelines (Lead Intake, Odoo Sync, Document OCR) are ready in sandbox preview within 48 to 72 hours. Full production deployments with custom validation rules typically take 1 to 2 weeks.

Where is our business data stored and processed?

Workflows run in fully isolated Docker environments on your self-hosted cloud infrastructure or dedicated private instances. Your data is never shared with public LLM pools or external vendors.

Do you support legacy databases and custom ERP instances?

Yes. In addition to Odoo 18, we build custom connectors for PostgreSQL, MySQL, REST APIs, GraphQL, and proprietary legacy databases using FastAPI middleware.
